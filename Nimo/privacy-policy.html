Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Nimo iPhone and iPad app, these support pages, and support messages sent to Song Yufei. Nimo does not require a Nimo account and does not upload your photos or recognized text to a developer-operated server for processing.

Images and local storage

The app processes images and text locally using device APIs. Imported originals, project previews, redaction regions, templates and preferences may be stored in the app’s local container. Imported originals can still contain the information you later cover in an exported copy.

The share extension temporarily stores selected images in a shared app container so the app can open them. Imported shared files are removed after being read, and expired shared files are eligible for local cleanup.

Permissions and system services

Nimo uses the system photo picker and, when you enable related actions, Photos access to open selected images, locate a recent screenshot, save edited images, replace an original, or perform a requested deletion. Clipboard access is used when you choose to import clipboard content. These permissions do not authorize developer access to your content.

Nimo does not provide its own cloud sync. Photos in iCloud Photos and app data included in your device backups may be handled by Apple according to your settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Apple processes Nimo Pro purchases and restoration. Nimo verifies purchase-entitlement information using StoreKit to enable output features. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles its own account, payment and transaction information.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. Preferences are stored with UserDefaults for app functionality, not tracking.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you voluntarily send. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot and maintain necessary support records. It is not used for advertising or sold. Please redact private content before attaching images.

Support records are kept only as needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant records or comply with legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent; applicable retention requirements may still apply.

This website

These static pages do not embed analytics, advertising, external fonts or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages, when used to host them, may process connection information such as IP addresses for delivery and security under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following external links subjects you to the respective provider’s policies.

Deletion and control

You can delete local projects from Nimo and manage templates in the app. Separate copies in Photos or Files must be deleted there. Uninstalling removes the app’s local container, but does not necessarily delete copies stored in Photos, Files or backups. Purchase records remain with Apple.

Children and policy changes

Nimo has no account registration and does not intentionally collect children’s information through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us. We will update this page and its date when our practices change.

Need help?

Email us with your app version, iOS version and a description of the issue. Redact any private details before sending attachments.
