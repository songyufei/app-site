Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Subly ("we", "our", or "the app") is a subscription tracking application for iOS. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Subly handles your information.

No Account Required

Subly does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app. There is no registration, no email collection, and no user profiles.

Data Storage

All subscription data you enter into Subly is stored locally on your device by default. Your data remains entirely under your control.

iCloud Sync (Pro Feature)

If you subscribe to Subly Pro, you may optionally enable iCloud sync. When enabled, your subscription data is synced across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is stored in your private iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not have access to your iCloud data.

Camera Access

Subly may request access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of scanning receipts using OCR (Optical Character Recognition). All image processing is performed entirely on-device using the Apple Vision framework. Images captured for OCR scanning are:

  • Processed locally on your device
  • Never uploaded to any external server
  • Never shared with any third party
  • Not stored beyond the scanning session unless you explicitly save the data

Photo Library Access

Subly may request access to your Photo Library solely to allow you to save exported statistics images (such as charts or summaries). The app does not read or access any existing photos in your library.

Notifications

Subly may send you notifications to remind you about upcoming subscription renewals. These notifications are entirely local and are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to any external server.

In-App Purchases

Subly offers in-app purchases (such as Subly Pro) that are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple and is not accessible to the developer. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles your payment data.

Third-Party Services

Subly does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks. The app does not contain any ads and does not track your usage behavior.

Data Sharing

Subly does not share, sell, or transfer any of your data to third parties. No personal data is collected by the developer.

Children's Privacy

Subly does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
